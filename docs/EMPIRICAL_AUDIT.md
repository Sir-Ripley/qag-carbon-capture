## Corrections the audit forced (we publish our misses)

1. **Over-unity energy:** asserting COP 1250× as an engineering result would trigger immediate
   preprint rejection. Peer-facing material uses conventional energy balances on renewable
   inputs; the QVR stack remains an exploratory theoretical horizon (Track B).
2. **CO₂ polymerization:** acoustic radiation pressure alone does not spontaneously polymerize
   CO₂ — radicals must be directed through transition-metal co-catalysts for C–C coupling.
   The architecture was updated to say so explicitly.
3. **f_kill band:** the disruptive frequency lands in the microwave C-band (~6.76 GHz), not
   the ultrasonic spectrum — described as an electrodynamic RF clearing signal operating
   *with* the 0.70 MHz acoustic field, not as acoustic resonance.
4. **QAG cosmology tensions** (documented for completeness): w_a sign discrepancy vs
   DESI DR1/DR2 (requires Einstein-frame treatment); unscreened Δg ruled out by atom
   interferometry (requires environmental screening); 3I/ATLAS acceleration explained by
   standard outgassing (used as an upper bound on vacuum forces instead).

## Publication strategy

Two manuscripts, split by archive standards — cosmology (gr-qc/astro-ph.CO) and
bio-engineering (physics.bio-ph/physics.chem-ph) are never combined in one paper.
Over-unity claims are replaced with rigorous mass/energy balances in all peer-facing
material. The dual-track presentation (Track A commercial / Track B theoretical) is the
public-facing implementation of this audit.
