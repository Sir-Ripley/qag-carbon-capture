# Formal Patent Claims

## Method and Apparatus for Hybrid Bio-Acoustic Carbon Capture via Isotopic Fractionation and Piezo-Photocatalysis

**Inventor:** Rodney Alan Ripley Jr. · **Status:** Patents pending

> Source: `claims.docx`, Drive folder *"Artificial Photosynthesis and Chemosynthesis"*.
> Transcribed faithfully; notation lightly normalized.

### What is claimed is:

**1.** A method for accelerating biological carbon fixation and inducing cellular
hyper-accumulation, the method comprising: a) cultivating extremophile microalgae within a
high-pressure, continuous-flow bioreactor, wherein the fluid medium comprises a stratified
dual-density liquid having a light-isotope upper biological suspension and a dense
deuterated base layer consisting essentially of deuterium oxide (D₂O); b) subjecting said
fluid medium to a hydrostatic pressure gradient ramping from approximately 15.0 MPa to
25.0 MPa over a predetermined operational window; c) generating an acoustic radiation field
within the fluid medium using at least one piezoelectric cavitation nozzle; and d) operating
said piezoelectric cavitation nozzle at a resonance frequency of approximately 0.70 MHz to
induce isotopic fractionation and localized cavitation, thereby forcing said microalgae to
over-accumulate stable carbon polymers while preserving enzymatic structural integrity
within the D₂O base layer.

**2.** The method of claim 1, further comprising the step of vector-mediated mutagenesis,
wherein recombinant DNA viral vectors, specifically formulated to upregulate Carbonic
Anhydrase II (CA II), are introduced into the fluid medium.

**3.** The method of claim 2, wherein the viral vectors are targeted and subsequently cleared
from the bioreactor by applying a highly specific, non-thermal electrodynamic disruptive
resonant frequency (f_kill) calculated based on the viral vector's molecular weight and
density proxy, ensuring the host microalgae's safety margin (Δζ ≥ 20.0 mV) is not violated.

**4.** The method of claim 1, wherein the application of the 0.70 MHz acoustic field operates
as a thermodynamic catalyst, increasing the localized system energy to accelerate CO₂
separation and adsorption kinetics across the carbon/water boundaries, thereby establishing
a restorative pressure that forces dissolved inorganic carbon species to aggregate.

**5.** The method of claim 1, further comprising a closed-loop frequency compensation control
system that dynamically adjusts the acoustic driver frequency (Δf₀(t)) in real-time based on
the shifting bulk suspension density (ρ(t)) of the hyper-accumulating microalgae to maintain
a substantially 98.0% acoustic impedance match.

**6.** An apparatus for piezo-photocatalytic carbon reduction and high-pressure acoustic
cavitation, comprising: a) a high-pressure bioreactor vessel configured to sustain internal
hydrostatic pressures between 15.0 MPa and 25.0 MPa; b) a fluid inlet for introducing a dense
deuterium oxide (D₂O) matrix and an organic substrate into the vessel; c) at least one
piezoelectric cavitation nozzle mechanically coupled to the bioreactor vessel, said nozzle
comprising an acoustic horn having an exponential cross-sectional area profile configured to
provide mechanical amplification; and d) a solid-state Interdigital Transducer (IDT) phased
array affixed to the base of the acoustic horn and configured to operate at a Tesla resonance
frequency of approximately 0.70 MHz.

**7.** The apparatus of claim 6, wherein the acoustic horn of the piezoelectric cavitation
nozzle is comprised of single-crystal Y-Z Cut Lithium Niobate (LiNbO₃).

**8.** The apparatus of claim 7, wherein the physical length (L) of the acoustic horn is
configured to be an exact integer multiple of the half-wavelength (λ/2) of the 0.70 MHz
acoustic wave traveling through the Lithium Niobate substrate, maximizing displacement at
the nozzle tip.

**9.** The apparatus of claim 7, wherein the tip of the Lithium Niobate acoustic horn
incorporates a micro-lattice doping structure comprised of oxygen-vacancy regulated Bismuth
Ferrite (BiFeO₃), wherein said doping structure is configured to couple acoustic cavitation
with piezo-photocatalysis to drive the separation of photogenerated and piezogenerated
electron-hole pairs for the reduction of carbon dioxide.

**10.** The apparatus of claim 6, further comprising a toroidal quantum vacuum resonator (QVR)
stack comprising a 24-node phased array mounted at a 12-degree geometric tilt, said QVR stack
configured to extract centripetal energy to provide a net electrical surplus sufficient to
power the bioreactor's high-pressure pumps and IDT drivers independently of an external
electrical grid.
